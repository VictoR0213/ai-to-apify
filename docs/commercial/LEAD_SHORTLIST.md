## Exclusions and limits

The research excludes resolved needs, sufficient existing workarounds, explicit requests not to receive third-party pitches, developer discovery posts, competing tools or service advertisements, failed primary-source verification, and adjacent synchronization problems outside the first offer. An advertised competitor price is supply evidence, not proof of a sale. Excluded records retain neutral IDs in the dataset rather than personal handles.

Keep n8n, Make and Hacker News forums read-only for this autonomous workflow. Other community or business routes also require route-specific permission and separate sending authorization. No messages, cloud jobs, purchases, monetization changes or schedules are initiated by the prepared package.

Qualify any future permitted opportunity using an authorized sample, current workaround, repeated cost, expected output and actual buying authority. A response, sample, trial, eligible paid use, settled earning and cash receipt are different evidence stages. The [14-day workflow](../COMMERCIAL_VALIDATION_14_DAYS.md) defines them; the [empty evidence example](../../examples/commercial-validation/evidence.example.json) records no successes.

# Commercial validation drafts

**Preparation only. Nothing in this file authorizes sending a message, publishing a post, changing billing, buying a service, launching a campaign, or creating a schedule.** The proposed window is October 21-November 3, 2026, Asia/Singapore; October 20 is Day 0 research. The window must move if actual readiness is delayed. All targets are hypotheses, and all drafts below remain unsent.

Customers still decide independently whether to provide a sample or buy. Never ask for API keys, passwords, payment details, production access, customer contact lists, or unnecessary personal information. Do not publish customer samples or private correspondence in this public repository. Keep any future private evidence storage separate, access-controlled, minimized, and subject to a defined retention period.

## Project value proposition

**Allowed use:** Draft project description for the owned GitHub repository or an owned landing page after publication is authorized. This wording may inform an Apify-specific listing, subject to its Store rules. It is not a solicitation for resale of AI quota or subscription access.

> Quota to Cash puts unused AI coding capacity to work building useful, verified data tools. Its local workbench, QuotaForge, turns a narrow JSON transformation into a bounded specification, checks it against independent expected outputs, and packages it as an Apify Actor. The experiment measures whether independent customers value the result enough to pay, then separates fees, costs, and actual cash receipts. Customer demand and profitability remain unverified.

## Customer-facing offer

**Allowed use:** An authorized owned landing page, a voluntary inbound reply, or a verified permitted business channel after the exact publication or reply is authorized. Make the actual delivery and price status visible before a customer acts.

## Five-question self-service intake

**Allowed use:** A future authorized owned intake or a permitted response to a prospect who requested information. Do not send unsolicited questionnaires. The deployed intake endpoint does not yet exist in this draft; do not publish an invented link.

**Introductory text:**

> This intake is handled by an AI assistant for VictoR0213's Quota to Cash project. Please use synthetic or fully redacted JSON. Do not include personal information, credentials, private URLs, or payment details. Submitting a problem description does not buy anything or authorize a paid run.

1. What recurring JSON cleanup task do you need, and what do you currently use to do it?
2. How often does it occur, approximately how many records are involved, and what specific problem does the current workaround cause?
3. Can you provide a small synthetic or redacted input and the exact output you want, including how duplicates, missing values, and invalid values should behave?
4. Do you authorize this AI-operated project to use the submitted sample privately for this evaluation, and may it reply through this same channel? Sample use and replies are optional; production data and public sample publication are not requested.
5. If the agreed fixture passes, would you consider a self-service trial at the proposed USD 0.01 per start plus USD 0.002 per delivered record, or prefer to decline? This price is a hypothesis and has not been activated by this plan.

Capture the voluntary answer and source reference rather than inventing a buyer persona. A researched company is not a qualified conversation. A sample without permission is not an authorized sample. Questions 4 and 5 do not authorize contact through unrelated channels or payment using someone else's account.

## Evidence and timing

The Day 14 report must distinguish successful delivery, eligible independent paid use, settled platform earnings, and cash actually received. Public Actor user counts, GitHub stars, free runs, and expressions of interest are not revenue. Preserve anonymized references for real evidence rather than storing private customer documents in the public repository.

The proposed October 21-November 3 window spans two earning months. The [payout documentation](https://docs.apify.com/actors/publishing/monetize/monthly-payouts) describes previous-month invoices on day 11 and payouts on days 21-25, subject to identity verification, thresholds, legitimate settled user payments, and processing. October and November usage can therefore map to November and December payout cycles. A 14-day validation does not promise cash receipt within 14 days.

Success is an initial signal of three independent eligible paying customers and one genuine repeat customer, with useful delivery and measured sustainable unit economics. Fewer than 20 qualified visits, fewer than five explicit qualified offers, unreliable attribution, or unresolved readiness means the result is inconclusive. Do not declare no demand from insufficient exposure.
